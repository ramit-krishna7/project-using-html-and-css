University Hostel Admission 2026

Safe, Comfortable and Affordable Accommodation

Important Information

Hostel admission is open for 2026-27. Students should apply before the last date. Rooms are allotted according to availability.

Available Hostel Options

Single Room

Private room with bed, table, cupboard and Wi-Fi.

Fee: ₹80,000/year

Double Sharing

Room shared by two students with basic facilities.

Fee: ₹60,000/year

Triple Sharing

Affordable room shared by three students.

Fee: ₹45,000/year

Hostel Facilities

  • 24-hour security
  • High-speed Wi-Fi
  • Clean drinking water
  • Mess and dining facilities
  • Laundry facility
  • Common study room

Important Rules

  • Carry university ID card.
  • Maintain hostel cleanliness.
  • Follow hostel timings.
  • Visitors must follow visiting hours.
  • Ragging is strictly prohibited.

Admission Process

  1. Fill the application form.
  2. Select your room type.
  3. Submit student information.
  4. University verifies the application.
  5. Receive hostel allotment.
